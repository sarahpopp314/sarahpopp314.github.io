# IU Astronomy

I have served as the graduate student Outreach Coordinator for the IU Astronomy Department for two years. In this role, I have worked with community leaders to organize various outreach events, including observatory tours, department booths at large STEM education events, star parties, and more. 

Want to organize an outreach event with the IU Astronomy Department? Email astoutre (at) indiana (dot) edu!

# PyIU: A Python Workshop Series to Forge Future Astronomers in Indiana

PyIU is a series of free Python workshops, developed and lead by IU Astronomy graduate students. The program was originally started to teach Python skills to undergraduate astronomy majors doing summer research, but we have expanded to other majors across IU and pre-college students in Southern Indiana. I am a founding member of the PyIU Organizing Committee. All of the materials we have developed for workshops are available on our [GitHub](https://github.com/BrandonRadzom/pyiu).
